Every gaper or Washington clam dug counts toward the limit — broken shell or not. Undersize clams with a size limit must be reburied immediately.

Legal Hours & Gear

½ hr before sunrise → ½ hr after sunset

Statewide, all clams. You can't even carry a digging tool on the beach outside this window. Allowed gear: spades, shovels, hoes, forks, rakes, clam guns, rigid pipe. No hydraulic pumps.

License

Required for anyone 16+, in hand while digging. Digital license via the CDFW app is fine.

Annual mussel-only quarantine runs statewide May 1–Oct 31 every year, separate from county-specific clam advisories. Cooking does not destroy PSP or domoic acid toxins. Discard the dark digestive meat regardless.

Pack List

Clam shovel or clam gun (narrow, deep — gapers sit 1–2 ft down)
Plastic 5-gal bucket — never galvanized, ruins the clams
Boots or waders for mud
Warm layers, gloves — mornings on the flats are cold
CA fishing license (physical or CDFW app)
Tide gauge if targeting size-limited species
